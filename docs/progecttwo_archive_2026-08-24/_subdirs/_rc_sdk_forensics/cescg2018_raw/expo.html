Hangar 13

A game development studio focused on creating AAA games for consoles and PCs, best known for Mafia III. Led by industry veterans, developing their own proprietary engine, there is much to learn from them.

Kunabi Brother

An independent game studio from Vienna dedicated to crafting beautiful and meaningful gaming experiences on mobile devices.

VR Group

For over twenty years we develop training simulations for crisis management and armed forces.

Procedural Design

A startup from Vienna offering a platform for massive creation of virtual worlds. It empowers collaboration, creativity and allows to produce content extremely fast.

Pygmalios

A real-time in-store analytics platform providing actionable business steps for retail.

Photoneo

Our vision is to give robots human-like eyes. We are going to change the whole definition of machine vision by introducing new hardware and software solutions in upcoming years.

Capturing Reality

RealityCapture is a software solution which automatically produces high resolution 3D models from photographs or laser-scans.

Figurama

3D media platform. Produces digital humans via integrated 3D scanning and motion capture system, bringing life into any 3D scene. Physical outputs include figurines, action figures and collectables.

VRVis

VRVis is Austria's leading research center for visual computing. It conducts basic and applied research as well as industry projects with focus on visualization, VR & AR, computer vision and AI.

Studio 727

We specialize in VFX and 3D for film industry and offer a complete production workflow, from production planning to the final product.

Nanographics

Nanographics, a spin-off by TU Wien, develops an award-winning tool for illustrative and multi-scale rendering of large biomolecular datasets.

Escape Motions

A small studio of creatives and coders dedicated to develop innovative visual tools.

Vectary

Create, share and customize 3D models online with easy-to-use 3D modeling tool VECTARY. Start 3D modeling today!

Do-it-yourself 3D printer

West Pomeranian University of Technology presents a "Do-it-yourself 3D printer". The low cost printer uses two individual extruders that can apply different filaments at the same time.
